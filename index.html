The problem

Searching for capacity should not become part of the emergency.

During emergencies, families may not know which nearby hospital has an available bed. Calling multiple hospitals wastes valuable time, while information about beds, departments, facilities and services can be difficult to access quickly.

01

Availability is unclear

Families may not know where a suitable bed is available nearby.

02

Time is lost on calls

Repeatedly contacting hospitals creates avoidable delays.

03

Information is fragmented

Bed, department and facility details are difficult to access quickly.

02

Our solution

A digital mediator between patients and hospitals.

Patients can search hospitals, check available beds and healthcare facilities, and request or book an available bed, subject to hospital confirmation. Hospital-side updates help keep information current.

+ Faster coordination — without replacing doctors or emergency medical services.
